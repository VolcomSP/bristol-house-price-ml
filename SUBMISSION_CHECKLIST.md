# Submission checklist

## Artefact package checks

- Run `python run_project.py --stage validate` in a fresh environment.
- Run the dashboard and test at least one valid and one invalid postcode.
- Confirm the research question and artefact scope still match the approved proposal.
- Confirm the repository or artefact link opens without requiring the marker to request access.
- Keep the selected model, dashboard data and documentation in their existing relative paths.
- Do not add the excluded national Land Registry files, raw Police files, backup folders or temporary files to the repository.

## Report and assessment checks

- Submit the 5,000–6,000-word report in the required Word or PDF format.
- Add the final repository or artefact link in the location required by Blackboard.
- Include the ethical approval form and supervision meeting record in the report appendices if required by the brief.
- Use UWE Harvard referencing consistently and verify every reference.
- Complete the AI-use declaration in the form and location required by the current assessment rules.
- Confirm the actual deadline on Blackboard because the supplied brief contained inconsistent issue and deadline dates.
- Check the final filename uses the required student-number convention.

## Viva preparation

Be ready to demonstrate and explain:

1. why all geography was standardised to 2011 LSOAs;
2. why Category A transactions were used for modelling;
3. why price was log-transformed;
4. how the chronological validation prevents test leakage;
5. what changed between property-only and enriched models;
6. why the lagged random forest was selected for the dashboard;
7. what the £99,655 MAE does and does not mean;
8. why the findings are predictive rather than causal;
9. the main data, bias and generalisability limitations;
10. how to reproduce the artefact from the included processed data.
